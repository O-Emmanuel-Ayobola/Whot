# Final Nigerian Whot Rules Specification

This release uses the supplied Nigerian Whot objectives/rules as the gameplay reference.

## Core
- First player to empty their hand wins the round.
- Match the active card by shape or number, or play Whot 20.
- Draw one from the Market when you do not play.
- Standard deck: 54 cards.
  - Circles: 12
  - Triangles: 12
  - Crosses: 9
  - Squares: 9
  - Stars: 7
  - Whot 20: 5

## Actions
- 1 Hold On: same player continues.
- 2 Pick Two: next player draws 2 and loses the turn.
- 5 Pick Three: optional house rule; when enabled, next player draws 3 and loses the turn.
- 8 Suspension: skips the next player.
- 14 General Market: every other player draws one; the 14 player continues.
- 20 Whot: wild card; player immediately names the required shape.

## Stacking
- Pick Two stacking is controlled by the room host.
- Pick Three stacking is controlled by the Pick Three setting.
- When enabled, 2 + 2 adds to the same pending Pick Two burden; 5 + 5 adds to the same Pick Three burden.

## Calls
- At two cards: Semi-last card.
- At one card: Last card.
- If a player fails to call Last Card before the next turn, another player can Catch them for two cards.

## Optional Perks
Secret Bounties and Shield / Peek / Swap are gameplay modifiers, not core Whot rules. The room host can enable or disable them before the first deal. The default is OFF for a clean classic table.

## House-rule variants shown in the tutorial
- Fire for Fire stacking
- Hold On chains
- Suspension chains
- General Market chains
- Whot over Whot shape override
- Clean finish / action-card finish variants
- Whot finish variants
- False Catch
- Touch & Follow
- Double Portion / multi-drop

Where a variant is not represented by a server setting, the tutorial treats it as a future/house-rule variant rather than silently changing the authoritative core game.
